About

Where Stanford science becomes companies.

BIOME is a student-led biotech incubator. We connect Stanford's engineering and life-science talent with the mentorship, structure, and capital it takes to commercialize a discovery.

01Why we exist

Stanford produces world-class biology and engineering. Too little of it leaves the lab.

The gap between a promising result and a fundable company is wide, and most students cross it alone. They have the science. What is missing is a clear path: how to find the right problem, de-risk the technology, and tell the story to people who write checks.

BIOME closes that gap with two tracks. Shark Tank is our incubator: a cohort of student teams builds toward a live pitch in front of investors. The Workshop Track teaches the lab and translational skills founders need along the way. A speaker series brings the people building biotech onto campus.

BIOME began as the Biological Interdisciplinary Open Maker Environment, a community for people who wanted to build with biology. Today, it leads bio-entrepreneurship at Stanford.

02Principles

How we operate.

  • 01

    Translation first

    We care about what happens after the result. Every track is designed around moving a technology toward patients, customers, and capital.

  • 02

    Builders first

    BIOME is for people who intend to build. Members work on their own ventures, against milestones, in front of investors.

  • 03

    Interdisciplinary by default

    Biotech companies need engineers, scientists, clinicians, and operators at the same table. Our teams are assembled that way from day one.
